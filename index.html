Recruitment Requirements

// Two recuitment paths

PATH 01The Standard Route

  • Role Check: Must actively have the official in-game TRT Commander role unlocked. SOC operatives automatically meet this requirement — joining as SOC counts.
  • XP Milestone: Must possess a minimum of 500,000 global XP combined across all plant roles.
  • The Recruit Test: Must achieve a passing grade on the entry exam.
  • The Evaluation Shift: Must complete a 30-minute monitored shift and finish placing in the Top 3 players out of the entire group being tested (if 5 or less people being tested on the shift must place as top 1 out of all TRT in the server).

PATH 02The Scholarship Route

  • Selection: Must be handselected directly by the Head of ETOG.
  • The Endurance Shift: Must complete a continuous 1-hour monitored combat shift.
  • Combat Ratio: Must maintain a definitive Kill-to-Death (K:D) ratio of 10:5 or higher.
  • Death Exemption Clause: Deaths only count if they occur during direct, active combat. Combat records are wiped of deaths caused by vehicles, or any deaths caused by WN camping.

Shift Operations & Schedules

// constant readiness protocol

Operational Doctrine

  • No Scheduled Shifts: ETOG operates on a state of constant readiness. There are no mandatory calendar times or forced group shifts. Members log on and secure the plant whenever they wish.
  • Shift Progression: To climb the ranks, players must voluntarily log performance metrics during sessions.
  • SOC Deployment: Members may deploy and complete shifts as SOC. SOC shifts count toward rank progression exactly the same as TRT shifts.
  • Civilian Deployment: Shadow Division operatives only — hand-selected members cleared to deploy as civilians during operations.

Ranking up requirements

  • 15-Minute Shift Benchmark: Must finish in the top 3 of the entire team OR secure at least 2 kills.
  • 30-Minute Shift Benchmark: Must finish in the top 3 of the entire team OR secure at least 5 kills.
  • 1-Hour Shift Benchmark: Must finish in the top 3 of the entire team OR secure at least 10 kills.
  • Endurance Shift (1+ Hours): Must maintain a strict average of at least 5 kills per hour, while keeping performance tight at a maximum of 5 combat deaths per hour (Standard exemption rules apply).
  • Notes: If you want to level up to either elite solo commander or elite commander you must submit a endurance shift of at least 1:15 min with averaging 10 kills per hour.
