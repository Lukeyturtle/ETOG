Elite Tactical Operations Group

ETOG // SECURING THE CORE, DOMINATING THE FIELD

ETOG is a premier, highly selective tactical community operating within the Naramo Nuclear Plant framework. We are a dedicated team of elite security combatants specialising in rapid response, perimeter defense, and absolute site lockdown. We operate entirely on player cooperation, merit, and high-tier tactical skill.

Recruitment Requirements

To join the ranks of ETOG, you must successfully complete one of the two clear vetting paths:

Path 1: The Veteran Merit Route

  • Role Check: Must actively have the official in-game TRT Commander role unlocked.
  • XP Milestone: Must possess a minimum of 500,000 global XP combined across all plant roles.
  • The Recruit Test: Must achieve a passing grade on the entry exam.
  • The Evaluation Shift: Must complete a 30-minute monitored shift and finish placing in the Top 3 players out of the entire group present on the server.

Path 2: The Commander's Exception

  • Selection: Must be handselected directly by the Head of ETOG.
  • The Endurance Shift: Must complete a continuous 1-hour monitored combat shift.
  • Combat Ratio: Must maintain a definitive Kill-to-Death (K:D) ratio of 10:5 or higher.
