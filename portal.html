Operations Briefing

// member intel — read before your shift

Shift Benchmarks

  • 15-Minute Shift: Top 3 of the entire team OR at least 2 kills.
  • 30-Minute Shift: Top 3 of the entire team OR at least 5 kills.
  • 1-Hour Shift: Top 3 of the entire team OR at least 10 kills.
  • Endurance Shift (1+ Hours): Average at least 5 kills per hour with a maximum of 5 combat deaths per hour.
  • Elite Promotions: Elite Solo Commander / Elite Commander require an endurance shift of at least 1:15 averaging 10 kills per hour.

Field Reminders

  • Death Exemptions: Deaths from vehicles or WN camping are wiped from your combat record. Only direct, active combat deaths count.
  • Constant Readiness: No scheduled shifts — log on and secure the plant whenever you wish.
  • SOC Deployment: You may deploy and complete shifts as SOC — they count toward rank progression exactly the same as TRT shifts.
  • Civilian Deployment: Shadow Division operatives only. If your profile shows the Shadow Division tag, you are cleared to deploy as a civilian.
  • Log Everything: Shifts only count toward promotion if you record them. Submit your shift report immediately after your session ends.
  • Honesty Protocol: Falsified shift reports result in removal from ETOG.
